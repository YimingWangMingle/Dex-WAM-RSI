The only retained demonstration arrays are the seven traces listed in `data/catalog.json`, containing 3,716 frames. Original numerical-file SHA-256 digests are recorded there. Both actions and public robot state are retained because commanded and actual contact poses differ. Timestamps are implicit at frame/30 seconds. Reference JPEGs are selected RGB frames, sometimes resized or annotated; each food view records its exact source frame, pose, and marked pixels in `data/food_views.json`. No MP4 or full training-corpus copy is included.

Food endpoints were manually identified and triangulated using matching measured wrist poses. Their ray residuals and parallax angles are preserved in the catalog. They are estimates of visible sausage-end centres, not object-centre ground truth. In the seven retained traces the estimates span approximately x=−0.351 to −0.248, y=−0.378 to −0.329, z=0.980 to 1.008 m; endpoint marking uncertainty is roughly 5–15 mm. The single z=1.0 plane is explicitly a fallback.

Deterministic OpenCV SIFT correspondences and NumPy/SciPy ray intersections checked static geometry across the corpus. Eighty-four examples yielded at least five consistent keypad points: median per-demo front x ranged from −0.27546 to −0.27387 m, with overall median −0.27513 m. Table/food-height estimates were less uniform and are not treated as a universal plane. Hand-pad/visual-mesh projections were compared with released wrist RGB to check quaternion order, the site's 180-degree rotation, and **public hand order**. Right arrays use ff/mf/rf/thumb; left arrays use rf/mf/ff/thumb, despite the XML actuator listing. In left-wrist 0015 frame 37 and 0000 frame 90, the rf-first permutation places fingertip meshes on the visible tips; ff-first misses laterally. Early action/state correspondence across the corpus supports preserving this same raw order in commands. Independent robot-only MuJoCo forward calculations validate the camera/hand transforms; no task scene is loaded. OpenCV features, geometric least squares, interpolation, and forward kinematics are numerical analysis, not trained task models.

`data/corpus_summary.json` records coverage and descriptive statistics. `data/validation.json` records offline package checks only. The package's recommended survey poses, food/appliance trajectory warps, chunk sizes, and recovery logic are engineering inferences. They have not been executed in a test episode. No original Zarr access, privileged state, evaluator code, hidden seeds, test results, future episodes, scoring thresholds, or hidden solutions were sought or used.
